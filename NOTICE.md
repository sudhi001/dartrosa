## ODK Collect test forms

`conformance/forms/collect/` contains test forms and media from ODK Collect
(https://github.com/getodk/collect, `test-forms/src/main/resources`),
Copyright the ODK Collect contributors, licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. They are used unmodified as conformance inputs.

## dartrosa_calendars

`packages/dartrosa_calendars` ports ODK Collect's non-Gregorian date picker
logic (Apache License 2.0) and the calendar algorithms of the libraries
Collect uses for them:

* Joda-Time 2.14 (https://www.joda.org/joda-time/), Copyright 2001-2024
  Stephen Colebourne, Apache License 2.0: `BasicChronology`,
  `BasicFixedMonthChronology`, `CopticChronology`, `EthiopicChronology`,
  `IslamicChronology`.
* persianjodatime 1.2 (https://github.com/mohamadian/persianjodatime),
  Apache License 2.0: `PersianChronology`,
  `PersianChronologyKhayyamBorkowski`.
* bikram-sambat 1.8.1 (https://github.com/medic/bikram-sambat), Copyright
  Medic Mobile, Apache License 2.0: `BsCalendar` and its month-length table.
* myanmar-calendar 1.1.1.RELEASE
  (https://github.com/chanmratekoko/myanmar-calendar), Copyright Chan Mrate
  Ko Ko, MIT License (based on Yan Naing Aye's Myanmar calendar
  algorithm, https://yan9a.github.io/mcal/, MIT License): the
  `MyanmarDateKernel`, `MyanmarYearConstants`, `MyanmarCalendarKernel`,
  `Thingyan` and `WesternDateKernel` algorithms and the Myanmar month names.
